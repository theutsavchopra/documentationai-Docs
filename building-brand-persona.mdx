---
title: Building Your Brand Persona on Bragly
description: How to Update Your Brand Context and Create a Brand Design Kit
---
Give your Bragly assets a consistent, professional identity. Setting up your Brand Kit and Brand Context allows Bragly to automatically customize your customer-facing forms, Widgets, Wall of Love layouts, and AI-generated review replies to perfectly reflect your unique brand voice.

### Prerequisites

- A registered [Bragly.io](http://Bragly.io) account with a Pro or Business plan

- Your official company logo file and primary brand hex colors.

- Your business website URL (optional, for Smart Context).

## Step 1: Build Your Brand Design Kit

Ensure all of your social proof elements match your business's visual branding.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1783160159622-vci45jj3ux-pasted-image-1783160153885.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1916" height="867" alt="" />

Navigate to the project homepage and click **Brand** on the left-hand menu.

Under the **Logo** section, drag and drop your image file or click **Browse** to upload it from your device.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1783161667376-lmqahmj8ru-Logo.Logo?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="797" height="368" alt="" />

Scroll down to the **Colors & Fonts** section to select your specific brand styling.

<Image src="https://assets.guidejar.com/uploads/cGxfwqjS3z6xmOPCVQtO/JORzRZHpW5YfHiRSXzSQAsjFu2u1/aea06ecf-402e-4b78-93f5-7864c0998e2a/1781961023268.jpeg" width="1920" height="1080" />

> ⚡ **Note:** Changes made to your colors and fonts are automatically saved in real-time.

To apply these visual elements across your live widgets and forms, simply click the **Apply Brand Kit** button.

## Step 2: Create Your Brand Context (AI Training)

Teach Bragly's AI about your business so it can write highly accurate, context-aware review responses and case studies for you.

### Option A: Manual Setup

Navigate to the project homepage and select **Settings** under the **Workspace** section in the left menu.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1783160674575-vkcnx6chlrr-Home-settings.Home-settings?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1896" height="787" alt="" />

Start by uploading **Your Company Picture.** If you need to update the **Project Name** at any point, this is where you do it.

<Image src="https://assets.guidejar.com/uploads/cGxfwqjS3z6xmOPCVQtO/JORzRZHpW5YfHiRSXzSQAsjFu2u1/ed2da785-0781-4f07-bc0d-c632a4a4a048/1781961074056.jpeg" width="1920" height="1080" />

Scroll to the **Brand Context** box and describe your company. Include details like your industry domain, area of expertise, and target customer personas.

Click **Update Brand Context** to save.

### Option B: Smart Context (Automated)

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1783161095490-xcrxo7i0sw-Smartcontext.Smartcontext?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="791" height="361" alt="" />

In the same **Settings** menu, locate the **Smart Context** section.

Enter your **Website URL** into the input field and click **Continue.**

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1783161196014-fnvrrsf4hxc-Smart-context.Smart context?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="796" height="365" alt="" />

Bragly will automatically scan your website to extract your brand persona and build your brand context.

> ⏳ **Please Note:** The Smart Context scanning process can take a few minutes to complete.

### Why this matters for your business

The next time you build a review widget or generate an automated response, Bragly will pull directly from this toolkit, keeping your online reputation seamless, professional, and entirely on-brand.
