---
title: How to Import Google Reviews on Bragly?
description: |+
  A step-by-step guide to pulling your Google reviews into your Bragly dashboard
---
Importing your existing Google reviews into Bragly allows you to instantly showcase your hard-earned reputation on your website and analyze your sentiment data in one central dashboard.

### Prerequisites

- An active [Bragly.io](http://Bragly.io) account.

- A live, public Google Business Profile (formerly Google My Business).

### Step-by-step Setup Guide

**Log In:** Sign into your Bragly account and click on the specific **Project** you want to manage.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1783167055152-pv1agbqvc3h-project-select.project-select?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1242" height="562" alt="" />

### **Navigate to Imports**&#x20;

On your project homepage, access the importer using one of two methods:

- Click the **Import Reviews** button under the *Getting Started* checklist.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1783167344963-9bfcoerskuj-Importreviews.Importreviews?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1085" height="446" alt="" />

- Or, select the **Reviews** tab from the left-hand menu and click **Import Reviews** in the top right.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1783167451101-t2bssubweli-importreviewsbutton.importreviewsbutton?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1081" height="372" alt="" />



**Select Google:** Click on the **Google** integration tile.

&#x20;

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1783167619818-2geff56nj2w-Select-Google.Select Google?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1251" height="567" alt="" />

**Find Your Business:** Start typing your business name into the search bar, then select your exact location from the dropdown menu. Click **Continue**.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1783168791670-44y4c21ht3i-Select-business.Select business?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1042" height="425" alt="" />

1. **Configure Filter Parameters:** Before importing, tailor which reviews you want to pull by setting your preferences:

   - **Sort By:** Choose *Rating*, *Relevance*, or *Recency*.

   - **Max Limits:** Adjust the total volume of reviews to pull.

   - **Minimum Rating:** Filter out low scores (e.g., only import 4-star and 5-star reviews).

2. **Fetch and Filter:** Click **Fetch Reviews** and wait a few seconds. Once loaded, you can:

   - Check individual boxes to manually curate which reviews make the cut.

   - Or, check the **Select all reviews** box at the top to bulk-select everything.

3. **Finalize:** Scroll to the bottom of the page and click the **Import \[X] Reviews** button.
