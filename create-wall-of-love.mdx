  How to create a Wall of Love on Bragly?

  A step-by-step guide to creating and customizing a dedicated testimonial
  showcase
---
A **Wall of Love** is an engaging, full-page or masonry grid showcase of your best customer testimonials. It builds instant social proof and credibility by giving potential customers a single destination to browse all your positive reviews, ratings, and success stories.

### Prerequisites

- An active [Bragly.io](http://Bragly.io) account.
- At least one project created with imported customer reviews.
- Brand Kit set up (optional, but recommended for automatic styling).\


### Create a New Wall

- From your project homepage, click **Wall of Love** in the left-hand menu.\

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1785587869296-1yt47rmr32s-walloflove1.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1251" height="572" alt="" />
- Click the **+ New Wall** button in the top right.\

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1785587964665-ekcbhom42es-WOL2NewWall.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1253" height="572" alt="" />
- In the setup window, enter your **Wall Name** and customize your **URL Slug** (example: user-reviews).\
  \
  _You can change the name as well as the URL slug later in the editor._
  <Image src="https://assets.guidejar.com/uploads/ee0c6e01-c106-471b-bbc1-c8b4f48534e8/JORzRZHpW5YfHiRSXzSQAsjFu2u1/59dbced6-7f6f-4c7a-b496-7504b43ef1d6/annotated/1781207040842.jpeg" width="1920" height="868" />
- Click **Create Wall** to launch the editor.

### Customize and Personalize Your Design

**Apply Branding:** Click **Apply Brand Kit** to automatically apply your primary brand colors, fonts, and logo.\


<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1785588067065-qaf1q00fhgb-WOL3Brandkit.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1256" height="570" alt="" />

**Select a Theme:** Browse the theme options to choose a layout structure (Grid, Masonry, Carousel, etc.) that best fits your aesthetic.\


<Image src="https://assets.guidejar.com/uploads/ee0c6e01-c106-471b-bbc1-c8b4f48534e8/JORzRZHpW5YfHiRSXzSQAsjFu2u1/f92fc06b-8ea0-4b9d-b212-237ae42077e5/annotated/1781207041242.jpeg" width="1920" height="868" />

**Configure Titles & Copy:** Your **Page Title** and **Subtitle** come pre-populated, but you can edit them to match your messaging.

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1785588994255-og0yzv5r6v8-WOL4-1CTA.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1918" height="870" alt="" />

**Add a Call to Action (CTA):** Enter a target link URL in the **CTA Button** field to direct inspired visitors straight to your pricing, checkout, or booking page.\
\
_Made a mistake? Click&#x20;_**_Reset_**_&#x20;at any time to revert to default styles._

### **Set Review Filters**&#x20;

Use the toggle controls in the left panel to curate your content:

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1785589080059-ir9y6gl7o0n-WOL5.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1916" height="871" alt="" />

- Limit the total **number of reviews** displayed.
- Set **minimum star ratings** (e.g., show 4-star and 5-star reviews only).
- Pin your favorite review.

Click **Save Changes** when you are satisfied with your layout.

### Preview, Share, and Embed

- Return to the main **Wall of Love** page from the left navigation panel.
- Click the **Preview (eye icon)** next to your wall to view the live preview.\

  <Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-ef2dab49-fbf1-4ba9-8607-f05685f04980/doc-cf544259-5222-49af-ad24-854dc546cabe/1785589396759-egvhd9k2pnf-WOL6.png?fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="1917" height="867" alt="" />
- Click the **Copy Link** button to grab your unique Wall URL. \
  You can:
  - **Embed:** Insert the URL/embed code into your website builder (WordPress, Webflow, Wix, etc.).
  - **Custom Domain:** Map the Wall of Love directly to your custom domain or subdomain.

There you have it! Your Wall of Love is live and ready to convert visitors!
